# Manager's Guide — Welcome to Fantasy Football!

This guide is for **Managers** — the people who run a team in the Peak Dragon
Fantasy Football League. It assumes you've **never played fantasy football before**
and don't need to know much about the NFL either. We'll explain everything from
scratch, step by step. Take your time, and don't worry — you can't break anything.

---

## 1. What is fantasy football?

Fantasy football is a game you play **using real NFL players**.

Here's the whole idea in a few sentences:

- You are a **Manager**. You run one **Team**.
- Your Team is made up of **real NFL players** that you pick (a quarterback, some
  running backs, some receivers, and so on).
- Every week, those real players go play their real NFL games. When your players
  do good things in real life — catch passes, score touchdowns — **you earn
  points**.
- Each week you're matched up against **one other Manager** in our league. Whoever's
  players score more total points that week **wins the week**.
- Do that for a whole season, and the Managers with the best records make the
  **playoffs** and play for the championship.

That's it. You're basically a coach/owner who picks players and decides who plays
each week, and the real NFL does the rest.

> **You never play football yourself, and you don't have to watch every game.** The
> site tracks what real players do and turns it into points for you automatically.

## 4. Positions & your roster

Football players play different **positions**. Each does a different job, and each
scores points differently. Here are the ones you'll draft:

- **QB — Quarterback.** Throws the ball. Earns points mostly for **passing** yards
  and touchdowns (and a bit for running).
- **RB — Running Back.** Runs with the ball. Earns points for **rushing** yards and
  touchdowns, and for catching passes.
- **WR — Wide Receiver.** Catches passes. Earns points for **receiving** yards,
  catches, and touchdowns.
- **TE — Tight End.** A mix of blocker and receiver. Scores like a receiver (catches,
  yards, touchdowns).
- **K — Kicker.** Kicks **field goals** and **extra points**.
- **DEF — Defense/Special Teams.** This is a whole NFL team's defense as one "player."
  It earns points for **sacks, interceptions, fumble recoveries, touchdowns**, and
  for stopping the other team from scoring.

### Your starting lineup

Each week you start this many of each position (this is our league's setup):

| Slot | How many | You put here… |
|------|----------|---------------|
| QB | 1 | a quarterback |
| RB | 2 | two running backs |
| WR | 2 | two wide receivers |
| TE | 1 | a tight end |
| **FLEX** | 1 | **any** RB, WR, or TE (your choice) |
| K | 1 | a kicker |
| DEF | 1 | a team defense |
| **Bench** | 5 | extra players, ready to sub in another week |

So you **start 9 players** each week and keep **5 on the bench** — **14 players
total** on your roster.

> **FLEX** is just a flexible spot. If you have three great receivers, you can start
> two at WR and the third in your FLEX. It gives you options.

**Only your starters earn points each week. Bench players earn nothing that week**
— but they're valuable as backups for byes and injuries (see below).

## 5. The Draft — building your team

The **Draft** is draft day: the one time everyone picks their players to begin the
season. It happens in the **Draft Room** page, live, with everyone at the same time.

### How the picking order works — the "snake"

Managers take turns picking one player at a time. We use a **snake draft**, which
just means the order **reverses each round** so it's fair:

- Round 1: Manager 1, 2, 3, 4
- Round 2: Manager 4, 3, 2, 1  *(reversed!)*
- Round 3: Manager 1, 2, 3, 4  *(and so on)*

That way, whoever picks last in one round picks first in the next. You keep picking
until everyone's roster is full (14 players each).

### Once a player is taken, they're gone

Every NFL player can only be on **one** team in our league. If someone drafts Patrick
Mahomes, no one else can have him. That's why the draft matters — you're claiming
players before anyone else can.

### Your pick timer

You get a set amount of **time per pick** (your Commissioner decides how long). A
countdown shows on your turn. Pick before it runs out!

### The Queue — your secret weapon

Before and during the draft, build a **Queue**: your private, ranked wish-list of
players you want. On your turn you can grab the top available player from your Queue
in one tap. Your Queue is **private** — no one else sees it.

### Auto-pick (a safety net)

If your timer runs out, the system can make a pick **for** you (if the Commissioner
turned that on): it takes the best player from your Queue, or the best available
player if your Queue is empty. And if you can't make it to the draft at all, the
Commissioner can put your team on **auto-draft** so it picks a full, sensible roster
automatically. **Tip: always build a Queue** so any auto-picks match what you'd want.

### Beginner draft tips

- **Draft starters first.** Get your QB, RBs, WRs, and TE early. Grab a Kicker and
  Defense near the **end** — you only need one of each.
- **Fill every position.** Make sure you can field a legal lineup (see the table in
  §4). The system helps, but plan for it.
- **Use your bench for backups.** Later picks are your depth for bye weeks and
  injuries.

## 6. Setting your lineup each week

This is the main thing you do during the season, and it's quick.

Each week, go to **My Team** and choose which players **start** (go in your lineup)
and which sit on the **bench**. Put a player in each slot from §4. Only starters
score.

### The weekly lock ⏰

Your lineup **locks** at kickoff time each week (the Commissioner sets it, usually
the first game of the week). **Once it locks, you can't change it for that week**, so
set your lineup **before** then.

### Why lineup choices matter — byes and injuries

- **Bye weeks:** every NFL team gets one week off during the season (a "bye"). A
  player on a bye **doesn't play and scores 0**. Don't start them that week — start a
  bench player instead.
- **Injuries:** if your star is hurt and won't play, start someone else.

Checking your lineup **every week** is the #1 habit of a good Manager. If you forget,
your lineup usually stays the same as last week — which is fine, unless someone's on
a bye or injured.

## 7. How scoring works

Your starters earn **points** based on what they do in their real games. Here are our
league's exact point values (this is called **Half-PPR** scoring — "PPR" means
**Points Per Reception**, and "half" means half a point per catch):

### Offense (QB, RB, WR, TE)

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Catching a pass (a "reception") | **0.5** each |
| Every 10 rushing or receiving yards | **1** (0.1 per yard) |
| Every 25 passing yards | **1** (0.04 per yard) |
| Rushing or receiving touchdown | **6** |
| Passing touchdown | **4** |
| Throwing an interception | **−2** |
| Losing a fumble | **−2** |

### Kicker (K)

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Field goal made | **3** |
| Extra point made | **1** |

### Defense / Special Teams (DEF)

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Sack | **1** |
| Interception | **2** |
| Fumble recovery | **2** |
| Touchdown | **6** |
| Safety | **2** |
| **Points allowed** (how many the defense gave up) | **+10** if 0 · **+7** if 1–6 · **+4** if 7–13 · **+1** if 14–20 · **0** if 21–27 · **−1** if 28–34 · **−4** if 35+ |

### A worked example

Say your running back has a good day:

- Rushes for **80 yards** → 8 points
- Scores **1 rushing touchdown** → 6 points
- Catches **3 passes** → 1.5 points
- Those catches went for **20 yards** → 2 points

**Total: 17.5 points** from one player. Add up all nine of your starters and that's
your team's score for the week.

### Live scores vs. final scores

- **During the games**, the site shows **live** scores that update as things happen.
  These are **not final** — they can still change.
- **A day or two after** the games, scores are **settled** to the official numbers
  and the week is locked in. Sometimes a score nudges up or down when the official
  stats come in. That's normal.

> Small note: because of how the official stats are published, **Kickers and
> Defenses** keep their live scores through settlement. Nothing you need to do — just
> so you're not surprised.

## 8. Matchups — winning your week

Every week you're paired against **one** other Manager. This is your **Matchup**
(see the **Matchup** page).

- Add up your nine starters' points → your team score.
- Whoever has the **higher score wins the week**. The winner gets a **win** (W); the
  loser gets a **loss** (L).
- If the league has an odd number of teams, one team gets a week off (a **bye**) and
  doesn't play that week.

You don't control the other Manager, and you don't play defense against them — you
just want your own players to score as much as possible.

---

## 9. Standings — the league table

The **Standings** page ranks every team, best record at the top. Teams are ordered
by:

1. **Win-loss record** (more wins = higher), then
2. **Total points scored** all season (as a tiebreaker).

Your record is written like **7-4** (7 wins, 4 losses). The regular season is
**14 weeks**. The Standings decide who makes the playoffs — so every week counts!

> The Standings count **regular-season** games only. Once the playoffs start, playoff
> results don't change your regular-season record.

## 10. Adding, dropping & trading players

After the draft, your roster isn't locked forever. You can improve it in two ways.

### Free agents (Add/Drop)

Any player **nobody owns** is a **free agent**. On the **Free Agents** page you can
**add** one to your team — but rosters are full (14), so you must **drop** one of
your players to make room. Adds are **first-come, first-served**: no waiting, no
bidding. If you want a free agent, grab them before another Manager does.

**Why add/drop?** To replace an injured player, pick up someone who's suddenly
playing great, or grab a backup before a bye week.

### Trades

A **Trade** is a swap of players with **another Manager**. On the **Trades** page:

1. You choose a Manager to trade with.
2. Pick the player(s) **you give** and the player(s) **you get**.
3. Send the offer. The other Manager sees it and can **accept** or **reject** it.
4. If they **accept**, the players swap automatically. If they **reject** (or you
   **cancel**), nothing changes.

Trades only work if **both** Managers agree — it's an offer, not a grab. Be fair and
friendly; good trades help both teams.

> **Trade deadline:** the Commissioner may set a week after which trading closes
> (like real sports). After the deadline, no more trades — so don't wait too long.
> Trades and free-agent moves open once the **draft is complete**.

The **Activity** page shows every add, drop, and trade in the league, so it's all out
in the open.

## 11. Playoffs & becoming champion 🏆

At the end of the 14-week regular season, the **best teams make the playoffs** — a
single tournament to crown a champion.

- The Commissioner decides **how many teams** make it (for example, the top 4).
- Teams are **seeded** by their regular-season Standings: the #1 record plays the
  lowest qualifier, and so on.
- The playoffs are **single elimination**: **win and you advance, lose and you're
  out.** Each playoff round is its own week, scored just like a regular week — set
  your lineup!
- Keep winning and you reach the final. **Win the final and you're the league
  champion.** 🎉

You can watch the whole bracket on the **Playoffs** page.

---

## 12. Your season at a glance

1. **Draft day:** pick your 14 players in the Draft Room.
2. **Each week (14 weeks):** set your lineup on **My Team** before the lock, then
   watch your players earn points. Win your Matchup to climb the Standings.
3. **Anytime:** add/drop free agents and propose trades to improve your team.
4. **Playoffs:** finish high enough in the Standings, then win your way through the
   bracket to the championship.

---

## 13. Top tips for new Managers

- ✅ **Set your lineup every single week** before the lock. This is the biggest thing.
- ✅ **Watch for bye weeks and injuries** — don't start a player who isn't playing.
- ✅ **Build a Queue before the draft** so you're never caught off guard.
- ✅ **Check Free Agents** now and then — a great player might be sitting there unowned.
- ✅ **Propose fair trades** — the best deals help both teams.
- ✅ **Have fun.** You'll learn as you go, and everyone starts as a beginner.

## 14. Quick glossary

- **Bye:** a week a player (or your whole team, if the league is odd-numbered) has
  off. A player on a bye scores 0.
- **Draft:** the event where everyone picks players to start the season.
- **Free agent:** a player nobody owns; you can add them.
- **FLEX:** a lineup slot you fill with any RB, WR, or TE.
- **Half-PPR:** our scoring — half a point for every catch, plus yards and touchdowns.
- **Lineup:** the starters you choose for the week (only they score).
- **Matchup:** your head-to-head game vs another Manager for the week.
- **PPR:** "points per reception" — points for catching passes.
- **Queue:** your private ranked wish-list of players for the draft.
- **Roster:** all 14 players on your team.
- **Snake draft:** a draft where the pick order reverses each round.
- **Standings:** the league table, ranked by record.
- **Trade:** a player swap two Managers both agree to.
- **Waiver / bidding:** *we don't use these* — free agents are just first-come,
  first-served.

Have fun, and good luck this season! 🐉🏈
